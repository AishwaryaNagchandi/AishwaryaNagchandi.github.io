01 — Beyond the Resume

The things
that make me,
me.

There is more to me than what fits on one page.

02

Outside the classroom.

Work is an important part of who I am, but it isn't the only place I've learned how to think, compete, create or collaborate.

Some of the things that have shaped me the most have nothing to do with a spreadsheet — and I think that's important too.

03 / 06

Building

I enjoy taking an idea and seeing how far it can go.

Project Prarambh gave me the chance to experience that from an entrepreneurial perspective. Building a startup concept around PET upcycled bottle filament meant thinking beyond the product — about customers, costs, capital, pricing and whether the business could actually sustain itself.

It made me appreciate the messy space where technology, finance and business meet.

04 / 06

Moving

I have always liked having something that challenges me outside academics.

I trained in Kathak and completed my Senior Diploma, and I have also played badminton at the district level.

They are very different disciplines, but both taught me something I carry into work: progress is usually repetitive before it becomes impressive. Consistency matters. So does showing up when you don't feel like you're improving yet.

05 / 06

Giving Back

Some experiences teach you leadership without ever giving you a leadership title.

Through community service at Govt. P.S. Baskhedi, I worked with 10+ volunteers on initiatives reaching more than 100 people and helped maintain records covering 500+ volunteer hours and donations.

It taught me that coordination is often less about being the person in charge and more about making sure everyone can do their part properly.

06 / 06

Exploring

I like learning even when there isn't an exam attached to it.

I have participated in elocution and debate, reached the national level in the International English Olympiad, and continue to enjoy music, museums, exhibitions and discovering things outside my usual field.

I don't think everything needs to become productive. Some interests are valuable simply because they make you notice the world differently.

07 — A Little More Context

I like having more than one way to look at the world.

Data helps me see patterns. Finance helps me think about decisions. Sport taught me competition and consistency. Kathak taught me patience and discipline. Music and art remind me that not everything meaningful can be reduced to a spreadsheet.
