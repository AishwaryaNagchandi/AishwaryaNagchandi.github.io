01 — The Path

From building with data to thinking about decisions.

My academic journey started with Data Science: learning how to work with data, build models and approach problems systematically. Finance added another lens — value, risk, returns and the logic behind decisions. Business Intelligence & Analytics brought the two closer to the questions organisations actually need answered.

02 — What Changed My Perspective

Different experiences taught me different things.

Research taught me patience and respect for evidence. Corporate work taught me adaptability, communication and the importance of context. Building projects taught me ownership — that an analysis becomes more useful when you have to explain what it means and what should happen next.
