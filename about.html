About Me

I'm interested in the space where analytics, finance and business come together — turning information into insight and insight into action.

01 — My Story

Three disciplines.
One perspective.

My academic journey combines Finance, Business Intelligence & Analytics and Data Science.

Data Science taught me how to work with data and build analytical solutions. Finance taught me to think about value, risk and returns. Business Intelligence showed me how analysis becomes useful when it answers the questions businesses actually care about.

Together, these experiences have shaped how I approach problems: understand the context, work with the data, challenge assumptions and translate the analysis into something actionable.

02 — The Intersection

Data gives me evidence.
Finance gives me perspective.
Business gives it purpose.

I enjoy problems where the answer isn't purely technical and isn't purely financial.

The interesting part is often the space between the two: understanding the data, recognising the financial implications and communicating what the analysis means for a business.

03 — Currently

I am currently pursuing a 5-year Integrated MBA Tech at MPSTME, NMIMS, Mumbai, graduating in 2027. The program brings together a B.Tech in Data Science and an MBA, with Finance as my major and Business Intelligence & Analytics as my minor.

It gives me the opportunity to build across disciplines — combining technical understanding with financial and business thinking, and shaping how I approach problems through data, context and decisions.
