02 — THE PATH

From building with data
to thinking about decisions.

My academic path began with Data Science. Learning to build models, structure data and question results gave me a strong analytical foundation. But it also made me curious about what happens after an analysis is complete.

Finance introduced another lens — value, risk, trade-offs and long-term thinking. Business helped me understand how those decisions play out through people, processes and priorities.

That is where my interests have come together: using data to understand a problem, finance to evaluate it, and business thinking to make the analysis useful.
