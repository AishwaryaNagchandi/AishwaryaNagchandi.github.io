01 — About

A little
context.

I'm curious about what sits underneath the obvious answer.

More than
one discipline.

A number can tell you what happened. A model can tell you what might happen. But understanding why something happened — and what someone should do about it — is what interests me most.

My path wasn't planned around one discipline.

I started with Data Science because I enjoyed solving problems through technology and analytical thinking. As I moved through my integrated MBA Tech, I became increasingly interested in Finance and Business Intelligence — not just how to analyse information, but how organisations use it to make decisions.

Today, I enjoy working in the space between the three — where data needs context, financial decisions need evidence and business problems rarely arrive in neatly labelled datasets.

02 — The Path
01

From building with data
to thinking about decisions.

My Data Science background taught me how to work with data, models and technology. My Finance specialisation taught me to think about value, risk and trade-offs. My business projects taught me that neither is useful in isolation.

The questions I find most interesting sit somewhere in the middle.

03 — What Changed My Perspective
02

Three environments.
Three different lessons.

IIRS — ISRO

Research taught me to slow down. Complex data does not reward assumptions. It rewards patience, investigation and respect for evidence.

Dow

Corporate work taught me to speed up. Problems are often incomplete, people are waiting for answers and you have to learn how to move forward while still figuring things out.

Projects

Projects taught me ownership. Once an idea becomes your responsibility, assumptions, numbers and outcomes stop being someone else's problem.

04 — Currently
03

Still learning.
Still building.

I'm currently pursuing an integrated MBA Tech at NMIMS, combining a BTech foundation in Data Science with an MBA Tech specialisation in Finance and a minor in Business Intelligence & Analytics.

That combination reflects how I naturally like to work: understanding the data, understanding the business problem and then thinking about what the analysis means financially.

I'm particularly interested in roles across analytics, business intelligence, finance, financial analytics and strategy — especially where the work involves solving an actual business problem rather than simply producing another report.
