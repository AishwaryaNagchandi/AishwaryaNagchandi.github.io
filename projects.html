01 / 05 — FINANCE × ENTREPRENEURSHIP
01

Project Prarambh

Evaluating the commercial viability of upcycled PET filament for 3D printing.

₹25L+ Projected capital requirements

The question

Could a sustainability-led manufacturing idea work as a financially viable venture? As co-founder and financial lead, I worked on its business and financial feasibility.

My analysis

Built a five-year integrated financial model covering capital needs, revenue scenarios, costs, WACC, NPV, IRR and payback, alongside target-market and scalability considerations.

The output

A decision framework for funding, pricing and long-term growth assumptions.

Financial Modelling · NPV · IRR · WACC · Entrepreneurship

04 / 05 — DATA × ECONOMICS
04

Budgets & Macroeconomic Outcomes

Examining long-run macroeconomic indicators and fiscal-policy patterns in India.

20+ years 150+ economic indicators analysed

The question

My analysis

Analysed more than 20 years of economic data and 150+ indicators, using trend comparisons and Excel dashboards to study different policy periods.
