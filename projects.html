02 — Finance × Entrepreneurship
01

Project Prarambh

Can the idea actually work?

Prarambh was a startup project around PET upcycled bottle filament for 3D printing. As a co-founder and financial lead, I worked on the part of the idea that is easy to overlook when you're excited about the product: whether the economics make sense.

My Role Co-founder · Financial Lead
Analysis Market research · Financial modelling · WACC · NPV · IRR · Payback
Output 5-year integrated financial model and investment assessment

I modelled capital requirements of ₹25L+, revenue scenarios and project economics, using financial metrics to understand whether the concept could be commercially viable and what would need to change for it to scale.

The project changed how I think about entrepreneurship. A good idea needs more than enthusiasm — it needs assumptions that survive contact with numbers.

03 — Business Strategy × Market Analysis
02

Nobelza

A good product still needs a market.

Nobelza focused on the market entry strategy for a portable TB-testing solution. The challenge was not simply understanding the technology, but understanding where it could create value and how it could realistically reach the people who need it.

My Role Market & Commercial Analysis
Analysis Market sizing · Competitive benchmarking · Demand forecasting · Financial modelling
Output Market entry, pricing and distribution strategy

I worked across market sizing, competitor analysis, demand forecasting and Excel-based financial and risk models for potential ₹5–10 Cr market opportunities.

The project taught me to look beyond the product itself. Technical value matters, but adoption depends on economics, customers, distribution and the environment around the product.

04 — Finance × Quantitative Analytics
03

Sectoral Analysis

Returns are only half the story.

I compared 10+ listed companies across the Indian renewable energy and pharmaceutical sectors to understand how performance, risk and market conditions interact.

My Role Quantitative & Financial Analysis
Analysis Ratios · Beta · Volatility · Sharpe Ratio · Sector Rotation
Output Comparative analysis, portfolio allocation and trading signals

The work moved beyond looking at returns alone. I examined volatility, risk-adjusted performance and portfolio implications to understand how the same return can mean something very different depending on the risk behind it.

It reinforced something I keep coming back to: numbers become much more useful when you understand the context surrounding them.

05 — Data Analytics × Economics
04

Budgets & Macroeconomic Outcomes

Numbers become more interesting when you put them in context.

This project examined more than two decades of Indian macroeconomic data to understand relationships between fiscal policy, economic growth and investment.

Scope 20+ years · 150+ economic indicators
Analysis GDP · Inflation · Fiscal Deficit · Capital Expenditure · FDI
Focus Fiscal policy and macroeconomic outcomes

Rather than looking at individual indicators in isolation, I tried to understand how they moved together and what larger patterns emerged over time.

The project strengthened my interest in using data not simply to describe what happened, but to understand the broader forces behind it.

06 — ESG × Business Analytics
05

Envaplax

Can sustainability reporting become more comparable?

I analysed 50+ corporate BRSR disclosures across 10+ ESG metrics to compare how companies communicate sustainability performance.

My Role ESG Data Analysis & Benchmarking
Analysis BRSR disclosures · ESG metrics · Benchmarking
Output Comparative Excel dashboards and reporting analysis

The project involved structuring disclosures, identifying reporting differences and building a comparative view that made the information easier to interpret.

It showed me another side of analytics: sometimes the value isn't in creating a sophisticated model, but in making fragmented information understandable enough for someone to use.
