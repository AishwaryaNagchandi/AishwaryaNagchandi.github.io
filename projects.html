02 / 06
Finance × Entrepreneurship

Project
Prarambh

Can a sustainability-led idea be turned into a financially viable business?

The Problem

Explored the commercial viability of converting recycled PET bottles into affordable 3D-printing filament while developing a scalable business model.

My Role

Worked as co-founder and financial lead, connecting operational assumptions with investment and funding decisions.

The Analysis

Built a five-year integrated financial model covering capital requirements, revenue scenarios, costs, WACC, NPV, IRR and payback analysis.

The Audience

Students, educational institutions, makerspaces, small businesses, startups, designers and hobbyists.

Output

A five-year financial model evaluating ₹25L+ in capital requirements and testing long-term investment viability through projected cash flows and financial metrics.

Financial Modelling WACC NPV IRR Market Analysis
