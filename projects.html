Project Prarambh

Finance × Entrepreneurship
Venture & Financial Model

The Problem

Can a sustainability-led idea be turned into a financially viable business?

Prarambh explored whether discarded PET bottles could be converted into affordable 3D-printing filament while building a business model capable of supporting the required investment, costs and long-term growth.

The Approach

As co-founder and financial lead, I approached the project from both a commercial and financial perspective. I helped translate the idea into a structured business model by identifying the target market, estimating capital requirements and developing a five-year integrated financial model.

I then used WACC, NPV, IRR and payback analysis to evaluate whether the projected returns justified the investment and to assess the overall financial viability of the venture.

How I Evaluated It

Capital Planning Estimated ₹25L+ in capital requirements.
Financial Model Built a five-year integrated financial model.
Investment Analysis Evaluated NPV, IRR, payback and WACC.
Market Strategy Considered students, educational institutions, makerspaces and small businesses as target users.
Investment Case
31.81% IRR
Project analysis evaluated an IRR of 31.81% against a 12.35% WACC hurdle.

Nobelza

Business Strategy × Market Analysis
Market Entry & Financial Analysis

The Problem

How should a portable TB-testing solution approach market entry in a complex and competitive healthcare market?

The challenge was to understand the commercial opportunity, assess the competitive landscape and determine how pricing and distribution could support market adoption.

The Approach

I approached the problem by combining market sizing, competitive benchmarking and demand forecasting to understand the opportunity from a commercial perspective.

I then developed Excel-based financial and risk models to evaluate potential opportunities and connect the market analysis with pricing and distribution decisions.

How I Evaluated It

Market Sizing Evaluated potential opportunities in the ₹5–10 Cr range.
Benchmarking Compared competitors and market positioning.
Forecasting Used demand forecasting to support commercial assumptions.
Financial Analysis Built Excel-based financial and risk models.
Go-to-Market Evaluated pricing and distribution approaches.

Sectoral Analysis

Finance × Quantitative Analytics
Comparative & Algorithmic Analysis

The Problem

How do different sectors behave from an investment perspective, and can quantitative signals support better portfolio decisions?

The project compared companies across the Indian renewable energy and pharmaceutical sectors to understand differences in financial performance, risk and market behaviour.

The Approach

I combined fundamental and market-based analysis, examining financial ratios alongside beta, volatility and risk-adjusted performance measures.

I then extended the analysis toward quantitative portfolio construction and algorithmic trading signals to explore how systematic approaches could support investment decisions.

How I Evaluated It

Company Analysis Compared 10+ listed companies across sectors.
Risk Metrics Analysed beta, volatility and Sharpe-based performance measures.
Quantitative Analysis Combined financial and market data to identify patterns across sectors.
Portfolio Strategy Explored sector rotation and portfolio allocation approaches.
Analytical Lens
Risk × Return
Moving from descriptive comparison toward quantitative investment decisions.
Financial Analysis Python Excel Sharpe Ratio Beta Portfolio Analysis

Budgets &
Macroeconomic Outcomes

Data Analytics × Economics
Longitudinal Data Analysis

The Problem

What can long-term macroeconomic data reveal about the relationship between government budgets and economic outcomes?

The challenge was not simply to analyse individual economic indicators, but to bring together multiple dimensions of India's economic and fiscal landscape and identify meaningful patterns over time.

The Approach

I worked with more than two decades of macroeconomic data covering growth, inflation, fiscal policy, capital expenditure and foreign investment.

I structured more than 150 indicators into a coherent analytical framework, allowing long-term trends and relationships between economic variables to be examined more systematically.

Envaplax

ESG × Business Analytics
Sustainability Benchmarking

The Problem

How can fragmented sustainability disclosures be turned into information that allows companies to be meaningfully compared?

ESG information is often spread across lengthy sustainability disclosures. The challenge was to structure that information into consistent measures that could support business benchmarking.

The Approach

I analysed Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reports and extracted relevant ESG information into a structured analytical framework.

I then used comparative analysis and visualisation to make differences across companies easier to interpret and communicate.

How I Evaluated It

Data Collection Analysed 50+ BRSR disclosures.
ESG Metrics Structured 10+ sustainability indicators.
Benchmarking Compared companies using consistent measures.
Visualisation Developed comparative dashboards to communicate sustainability insights.
