Finance × Entrepreneurship

Project Prarambh

Can a sustainability-led idea be turned into a financially viable business?

The Problem

The project explored the commercial viability of converting recycled PET bottles into affordable 3D-printing filament while creating a scalable business model.

My Role

Worked as co-founder and financial lead, connecting operational assumptions with investment decisions.

The Analysis

Built a five-year integrated financial model covering capital requirements, revenue scenarios, costs and investment viability using WACC, NPV, IRR and payback analysis.

The Audience

Students, educational institutions, makerspaces, small businesses, startups, designers and hobbyists.

Financial perspective

The model evaluated ₹25L+ in capital requirements and tested the project’s ability to create long-term value through projected cash flows and investment metrics.

Strategy × Market Analysis

Nobelza

How can a portable healthcare testing solution find a commercially viable path into the market?

The Problem

The project explored market entry for a portable TB-testing solution, with a focus on demand, competition and commercialisation.

My Role

Led market-entry analysis and connected market assumptions with financial and operational considerations.

The Analysis

Used market sizing, competitive benchmarking, demand forecasting, pricing and distribution analysis, supported by Excel-based financial and risk models.

The Opportunity

Modelled potential opportunities in the ₹5–10 Cr range and assessed how commercial choices could affect deployment efficiency.

Strategic perspective

The analysis connected market evidence with pricing, distribution and risk considerations to build a more practical commercialization case.
