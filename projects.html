01 / 05

Project Prarambh

Finance × Entrepreneurship
Venture & Financial Model

The Problem

How can discarded PET bottles be transformed into a commercially viable product while remaining affordable for the people who use it?

The Approach

As co-founder and financial lead, I worked on the financial and commercial side of a venture converting recycled PET bottles into affordable 3D-printing filament.

I developed a five-year integrated financial model, evaluated capital requirements and assessed the investment case using valuation and capital budgeting techniques.

What I Worked With

Capital Planning Estimated ₹25L+ in capital requirements.
Financial Model Built a five-year integrated financial model.
Investment Analysis Used NPV, IRR, payback and WACC to evaluate the investment case.
Strategy Considered students, educational institutions, makerspaces and small businesses as target users.
Investment Case
31.81% IRR
Project analysis evaluated an IRR of 31.81% against a 12.35% WACC hurdle.
Financial Modelling NPV IRR WACC Entrepreneurship
02 / 05

Nobelza

Business Strategy × Market Analysis
Market Entry & Financial Analysis

The Problem

How should a portable TB-testing solution approach market entry, pricing and distribution in a complex healthcare market?

The Approach

I worked on the market-entry side of the project, combining market sizing, competitive benchmarking and demand forecasting to understand the commercial opportunity.

Financial and risk models were then used to evaluate potential market opportunities and inform pricing and distribution decisions.

What I Worked With

Market Sizing Evaluated potential opportunities in the ₹5–10 Cr range.
Benchmarking Compared competitors and market positioning.
Forecasting Used demand forecasting to support commercial assumptions.
Financial Analysis Built Excel-based financial and risk models.
Go-to-Market Evaluated pricing and distribution approaches.
Focus
Market Entry
A project at the intersection of business strategy, financial analysis and market intelligence.
Market Research Financial Modelling Risk Analysis Forecasting Strategy
03 / 05

Sectoral Analysis

Finance × Quantitative Analytics
Comparative & Algorithmic Analysis

The Problem

How do different Indian sectors behave from an investment perspective, and can quantitative signals support better portfolio decisions?

The Approach

I compared companies across the renewable energy and pharmaceutical sectors using financial ratios, risk measures and market performance indicators.

The analysis extended into quantitative portfolio construction and algorithmic trading signals to explore how data-driven approaches could support investment decisions.

What I Worked With

Company Analysis Compared 10+ listed companies across sectors.
Risk Metrics Analysed beta, volatility and Sharpe-based performance measures.
Quantitative Analysis Combined financial and market data to identify patterns across sectors.
Portfolio Strategy Explored sector rotation and portfolio allocation approaches.
Lens
Risk × Return
Using quantitative evidence to move from descriptive comparison toward investment decisions.
Financial Analysis Python Excel Sharpe Ratio Beta Portfolio Analysis

Budgets &
Macroeconomic Outcomes

Data Analytics × Economics
Longitudinal Data Analysis

The Problem

What can long-term macroeconomic data reveal about the relationship between government budgets and economic outcomes?

The Approach

I worked with more than two decades of macroeconomic data, bringing together indicators across growth, inflation, fiscal policy, capital expenditure and foreign investment.

The objective was to structure a large set of indicators into a form that could support meaningful trend analysis and comparison.

What I Worked With

Indicators Worked with 150+ economic and fiscal indicators.
Variables GDP, inflation, fiscal deficit, capex and FDI.
