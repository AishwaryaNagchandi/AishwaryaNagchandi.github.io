How I Apply Them

From question to decision.

I generally approach analytical problems as a sequence rather than starting with a tool.

01 — Understand

Clarify the business question and what decision the analysis needs to support.

02 — Structure

Identify the relevant data, assumptions, constraints and analytical approach.

03 — Analyse

Use the appropriate quantitative, financial or analytical methods to identify patterns and evidence.

04 — Communicate

Translate the analysis into a form that is clear and useful to the people consuming it.

05 — Decide

Connect the findings back to the original question and the action that follows.

The principle

Good analysis should make the next decision easier.

I am still building depth across these areas, but the common thread is simple: I want the work I do to be useful, understandable and connected to a real decision.
