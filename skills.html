08 — HOW I WORK

Understand first.
Then analyse.

I have found that having the right tool is only one part of good analysis. The more important part is understanding the question and knowing how the result will be used.

01 Understand

What is the problem, and why does it matter?

02 Structure

Break the problem into questions that can actually be answered.

03 Analyse

Choose the data, methods and tools that fit the question.

04 Communicate

Turn the findings into clear analysis, dashboards or consulting-style presentations.

05 Act

Connect the analysis to a practical next step.

LET’S CONNECT

The tool changes.
The approach stays.

I am constantly learning new tools, but I care just as much about how I use them — whether that means building a model, creating a Power BI dashboard, exploring data in Tableau or Looker, analysing a dataset in Python, or presenting an idea clearly to a business audience.
