05 — What I Took Away

The tools matter. The thinking matters more.

01

Structure before analysis.

Whether the input is a business process or a scientific dataset, creating structure makes complex problems easier to understand.

02

Context changes the answer.

Numbers only become useful when understood in the context of the people, processes and decisions behind them.

03

Communication completes analysis.

A strong insight has little value if it cannot be understood and acted upon by the people who need it.
