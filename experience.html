RESEARCH × BUSINESS × DATA

My experiences have taken me from a research environment at IIRS–ISRO to my first corporate role at Dow, with a few very different experiences in between.

Each one taught me something different — about data, people, responsibility and the way I approach problems.

01 — THE THREAD

Every experience taught me something different.

I don't see my experiences as separate roles on a timeline. Each one added something to how I work today.

At IIRS–ISRO, I learned what it feels like to work alongside people with much more experience than me. At Dow, I learned how to work in a corporate environment for the first time.

My freelance work at Soul AI taught me that putting in the effort to improve can lead to greater responsibility. And while working with children at Government P.S. Baskhedi, I learned to create useful solutions with whatever resources were available.

The environments were different, but the lessons were connected: stay curious, learn quickly, take responsibility and find a way forward.

Remote Sensing & Hyperspectral Data Analytics

IIRS–ISRO was one of my earliest experiences with research in a professional setting. I worked with hyperspectral and geospatial data, using Python and machine-learning techniques to study spectral patterns and classify materials.

What made the experience especially memorable was the environment I was working in. I was a second-year student and the youngest person in the research group, surrounded by doctoral and postdoctoral researchers.

I had a lot to learn, and being in that environment pushed me to learn quickly and become comfortable asking questions.

Data Workflows

Machine Learning

Explored machine-learning approaches for material-level classification.

Learned to communicate technical work clearly in a research environment.

2024

Government P.S. Baskhedi

SUMMER HELPER · COMMUNITY SERVICE

I worked with underprivileged children and created simple learning activities using materials that were already available around them.

We turned tablet scraps into counting tools, used rocks to introduce colours and created other learning activities through a best-out-of-waste approach.

Instead of starting with what was missing, I learned to look at what was already available and think about how it could be used differently.

What I learned: Resourcefulness starts with understanding the situation. Sometimes the best solution is not about having more resources, but about using the ones you already have creatively.
